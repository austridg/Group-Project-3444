## Project Name:
Centsible

## Description:
Centsible is a personal finance tracker that helps individuals who are serious about where their money is going. The goal is to track users' income and expenses and, with that information, help generate an idea of what kind of budget they should follow, spending habits and flexibility, and staying on top of bills/subscriptions.
